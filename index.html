      The rules, in plain English

      Five rules, each based on published research. Stocks are bought and sold the same day. Crypto trades around the clock.

      1. Opening-Range Breakout (stocks)

      Watch the first 5 minutes after 9:30 AM. If buyers won that candle on heavy volume, buy when the price breaks above that candle's high and stays above VWAP. Sell by 3:50 PM.

      Zarattini & Aziz, "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" (2023)

      2. VWAP Trend (SPY, QQQ)

      VWAP is the average price everyone paid today. Buy when the price is above it and the short-term trend is up; sell when a 5-minute candle closes back below it.

      Zarattini & Aziz, "VWAP: The Holy Grail for Day Trading Systems" (2023)

      3. Late-Day Momentum (SPY)

      If the first half hour of the day and the 3:00 to 3:30 stretch were both up, buy SPY around 3:30 and sell at 3:55. The last half hour tends to follow the first.

      Gao, Han, Li & Zhou, "Market Intraday Momentum," Journal of Financial Economics (2018)

      4. Crypto Breakout

      In an uptrend, buy when a 15-minute candle closes above the 24-hour high on big volume. A trailing stop follows the price up; sell within 24 hours.

      Bitcoin intraday time-series momentum research; Quantpedia, "Trend-following and Mean-reversion in Bitcoin" (2022)

      5. Crypto Dip-Buy

      In an uptrend, buy when the coin gets oversold (RSI under 30) and starts bouncing. Fixed profit target and stop; sell within 12 hours.

      Short-term mean reversion, same Bitcoin study

      Money rules

      Each trade risks about 1.5% of the account if its stop hits. One trade can use up to the whole $100. At most 3 trades open at once. Down 5% on the day means no new trades until tomorrow.

      Paper trades still pay realistic costs: 0.02% slippage on stocks, 0.40% per side on crypto.

      Good to know before you copy a trade

      • This is pretend money. Nothing here is financial advice, and past results don't predict future ones.
      • Most day traders lose money. In a study of nearly 20,000 Brazilian day traders who kept at it for 300+ days, 97% lost money (Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti, 2019). An independent replication of the opening-range breakout found its edge mostly disappears after trading costs. That's why this page shows every loss and every fee.
      • Timing: the bot checks the markets about every 5 minutes (GitHub sometimes runs it a few minutes late). If you see a trade late and the price has run past the "skip it if" number, skip it.
      • Prices: stock prices come from Yahoo Finance and crypto from Coinbase, via free public feeds.
      • Real accounts: brokers have their own rules for frequent day trading in small accounts. Check yours before trading real money.
